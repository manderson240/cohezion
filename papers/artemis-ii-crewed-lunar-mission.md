## Summary
Artemis II will carry four astronauts on a free-return trajectory around the Moon — the first crewed lunar mission since Apollo 17 in 1972 — testing life support, navigation, and communications systems for eventual sustained lunar presence.

## Key Abstractions
Launching no earlier than February 6, 2026 aboard NASA's Space Launch System, the mission marks historic firsts: first Black astronaut to travel to the Moon (Victor Glover), first woman in deep space (Christina Koch), and first Canadian on a lunar mission (Jeremy Hansen). The ~10-day free-return trajectory tests Orion spacecraft systems as a precursor to Artemis III lunar landing.
