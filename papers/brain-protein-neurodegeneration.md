## Abstract

Recent research reveals that amyloid beta interacts with immune cell receptors in the brain, triggering metabolic changes in microglia that affect amyloid clearance. The complex interplay between immune response, metabolism, and amyloid pathways plays a central role in neurodegeneration.

## Key Findings

- Amyloid beta interaction with immune cells causes a metabolic switch from oxidative phosphorylation to glycolysis, which chronically breaks down both pathways
- Effective amyloid clearance requires balanced microglial metabolic states that protect against amyloid toxicity
- Neuroinflammation involves microglia switching from phagocytosis of pathological aggregates to neurotoxic killing as tau tangles form
- Lipid metabolism alterations and immune dysregulation are interconnected pathophysiological mechanisms in Alzheimer's disease progression
