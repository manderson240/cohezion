# TongGeometry: AI System for IMO-Level Geometry

Chinese neuro-symbolic AI system that outperforms Google DeepMind's AlphaGeometry at IMO-level geometry problems.

## Key Findings

- Developed by Beijing Institute for General AI and Peking University
- Not just a solver — uniquely generates mathematical problems via principled tree search
- Three generated problems appeared in real competitions (2024 Beijing National HS Math League, 2024 US Ersatz Math Olympiad)
- AlphaGeometry solved only 3 of TongGeometry's 10 proposals
- Takes less than half the time and uses simpler computational resources than US competitors
