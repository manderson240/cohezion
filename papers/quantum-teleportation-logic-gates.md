# Quantum Teleportation of Logic Gates Between Computers

## Summary

Oxford researchers demonstrated teleportation of quantum logic gates between two separate quantum processors linked via a photonic network interface, achieving 86% accuracy. Published in Nature.

## Key Findings

- Two quantum processors were linked six and a half feet apart using a photonic network interface with no physical wires.
- Quantum gates (fundamental computing operations) were teleported from one module to the other.
- Validated using Grover's Algorithm for searching unsorted datasets across multiple modules.
- Demonstrates that modular quantum systems can be merged into a single fully connected quantum computer.

## Implications

Lays groundwork for a future quantum internet where distant processors form ultra-secure networks for communication, computation, and sensing.
