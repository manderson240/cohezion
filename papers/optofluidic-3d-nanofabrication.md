# Optofluidic 3D Micro/Nanofabrication

## Summary

Nature paper introduces a universal 3D micro/nanofabrication strategy that uses femtosecond-laser-induced heating to create localized thermal gradients, enabling precise spatiotemporal control over optofluidic interactions in nanoparticle dispersions.

## Key Findings

- Femtosecond laser creates a localized heating spot that generates thermal gradients for precise assembly control.
- Compatible with diverse nanoparticle compositions: metals, metal oxides, carbon nanomaterials, and quantum dots.
- Enables rapid, highly localized assembly into complex volumetric 3D microstructures and nanostructures.
- Universal strategy -- works across a broad range of materials.
