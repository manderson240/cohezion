# Silicon as the Bedrock of Quantum Computers

The 14|15 platform combines crystalline silicon substrates with phosphorus atom qubits for quantum computing.

## Key Findings

- Spin qubits in high-purity silicon exhibit long coherence times due to reduced charge and magnetic noise
- Silicon Quantum Computing launched **Quantum Twins** — an application-specific quantum simulator using atomically precise silicon qubit arrays
- Up to 15,000 qubit registers patterned on pure silicon with 0.13nm accuracy
- Demonstrated patterning 250,000 qubit registers in eight hours (Nov 2025)
