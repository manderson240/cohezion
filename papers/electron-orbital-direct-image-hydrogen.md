## Summary
For the first time, physicists directly captured a visual image of an electron's orbital within a hydrogen atom, crossing the century-old boundary between quantum probability distributions and tangible physical observation.

## Key Abstractions
For over a century electrons existed only as probability clouds — mathematical abstractions. This direct imaging collapses the gap between quantum theory and physical reality, revealing the electron's spatial orbital as a visible, intimate portrait rather than an equation. The technique likely uses attosecond laser pulses or tunneling microscopy to resolve orbital structure at atomic scales.
