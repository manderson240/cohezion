# Axion Dark Matter Constraints via Distributed Quantum Sensors

## Summary

Published in Nature, this experiment uses five correlated noble-gas laboratory setups across two cities to monitor potential transient spin rotations from topological defect dark matter (TDM) interactions, reaching sensitivity of ~10^-6 rad.

## Key Findings

- Ultralight axion particles could form stable macroscopic field configurations as topological defect dark matter
- Five noble-gas labs in two cities correlated to detect TDM-induced spin rotations
- Uses amplification and optimal noise filtering in hyperpolarized noble-gas spins
- Achieved ~10^-6 rad sensitivity to transient polarized spin rotations
- Demonstrates distributed quantum sensing network approach
