## Abstract

Observations using the Southern African Large Telescope (SALT) resolved the mystery of WOH G64, the most luminous red supergiant in the Large Magellanic Cloud. Despite disappearing from view in 2024 due to a newly formed dust cloud, SALT spectroscopy reveals the star remains a red supergiant with a hot companion whose gravitational interaction triggered the mass loss event.

## Key Findings

- WOH G64 appeared to vanish in 2024 due to newly formed dust cloud, initially suggesting evolution into rare yellow hypergiant phase
- SALT optical spectra from November 2024-December 2025 reveal clear molecular absorption bands from Titanium Oxide (TiO), confirming red supergiant status
- Binary system with hot companion star (component B) whose gravitational influence stretches red supergiant atmosphere
- Companion's approach triggered heavy mass loss and dust cloud formation that obscured primary star and ionized surrounding gas
- Resolves speculation about stellar evolution phase change, demonstrating sophisticated interaction in close binary system
