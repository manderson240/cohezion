# Superfluid-to-Supersolid Phase Transition Observed

## Summary

Physicists from Columbia University and UT Austin observed for the first time a superfluid coming to a standstill, undergoing a phase transition to what appears to be a supersolid -- a quantum state that is simultaneously crystalline and frictionless.

## Key Findings

- First observation of superfluid-to-supersolid phase transition
- Used excitons in graphene (single-atom carbon sheets)
- At high density, excitons behaved as superfluid; at decreased density, they stopped and became insulators
- Supersolids are both liquid-like and solid-like simultaneously
- Resolves a half-century-old question about what happens when superfluids are further cooled
- Led by Cory Dean (Columbia) and Jia Li (UT Austin)
