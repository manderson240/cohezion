# DNA Origami for Precise Molecular Patterning on 2D Semiconductors

## Summary

Skoltech, LMU Munich, Nanjing University, and NIMS Japan developed a method for depositing organic dye molecules on monolayer molybdenum disulfide using DNA origami nanostructures (~100nm) that carry dye molecules at predefined positions.

## Key Findings

- DNA origami structures serve as templates for nanoscale-precise placement of organic molecules on 2D semiconductors.
- Achieves controlled Forster resonance energy transfer (FRET) between the dye molecules and the semiconductor.
- Enables structuring semiconductor properties at the nanoscale through the deposited pattern.
- Potential applications in advanced nanoelectronic and nanophotonic devices.
