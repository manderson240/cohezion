# Switching Graphitic Polytypes in Nanocavities

## Summary

Published in Nature Nanotechnology, this research demonstrates fully reversible transformations of Bernal tetralayers to rhombohedral crystals at 30nm scale, using sub-nanonewton lateral shear forces and sub-femtojoule energy per switching event.

## Key Findings

- Reversible polytype switching achieved at nanometer scale
- Uses intentionally misaligned spacer patterned with nanometre-scale cavities between aligned bilayers
- Active bilayers form stable single-domain polytypes within cavities
- Outside cavities, layers slide freely over superlubric incommensurate interfaces with ultralow friction
- Energy cost: less than 1 femtojoule per switching event
