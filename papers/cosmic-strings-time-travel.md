# Cosmic Strings and Time Travel

## Summary

Physicists theorize that cosmic strings -- one-dimensional topological defects formed during phase transitions after the Big Bang -- could create closed timelike curves enabling time travel if two infinite parallel strings pass each other.

## Key Findings

- Cosmic strings are "scars" in spacetime fabric from rapid early-universe expansion
- J. Richard Gott (Princeton, 1991) proposed two parallel cosmic strings warping spacetime into closed timelike curves
- Ken Olum (Tufts) confirms traveling around such strings could return traveler to earlier time
- No cosmic strings have been directly observed yet
- Vera C. Rubin Observatory may enable detection by surveying 17 billion previously unseen stars
