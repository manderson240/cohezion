# Super-Earth Magnetic Protection from Basal Magma Oceans

## Summary

University of Rochester researchers found that deep basal magma oceans (BMOs) in super-Earths can generate protective magnetic fields lasting billions of years, potentially making these planets more habitable than previously thought.

## Key Findings

- Under extreme pressure, iron-rich magma becomes metallic and electrically conductive
- Super-Earths (3-6x Earth mass) could maintain BMO-driven magnetic fields for several billion years
- These fields may be longer-lived and stronger than Earth-like metallic core dynamos
- Magnetic shields essential for preventing atmospheric stripping by stellar winds
- Laser shock experiments at URochester's Laboratory for Laser Energetics confirmed conductivity
- Published in Nature Astronomy
