## Abstract

Applied Compute, founded by three former OpenAI researchers, is in talks to raise new funding at a $1.3 billion valuation, more than doubling its previous $500 million valuation in less than three months. The enterprise-focused AI startup specializes in customizing language models with proprietary customer data and has already raised $80 million from top-tier investors.

## Key Findings

- Applied Compute in early-stage talks to raise up to $70 million at $1.3 billion valuation, led by potential investor Kleiner Perkins
- Founded by three former OpenAI researchers with significant credentials: o1 reasoning model contributor, agentic software systems expert, and ML infrastructure specialist
- Previously raised $80 million from Sequoia Capital, Benchmark, and Lux Capital in initial rounds
- Valuation more than doubled from $500 million in previous funding discussions to $1.3 billion in less than three months
- Enterprise focus on model customization represents significant market opportunity in proprietary AI customization
