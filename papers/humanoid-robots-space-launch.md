## Abstract

Multiple organizations are preparing humanoid robots for space deployment in 2026. Chinese firm Engine AI is partnering to launch the first robot astronaut, while SpaceX/Tesla plan to land Optimus robots on Mars, marking a breakthrough year for commercial humanoid robotics in space exploration.

## Key Findings

- Engine AI partnered with Beijing Interstellar Human Spaceflight Technology to develop PM01, a general-purpose humanoid robot astronaut with advanced sensors and autonomous decision-making
- A Chinese humanoid robot successfully connected to a GalaxySpace satellite in a world-first demonstration, achieving synchronous transmission of visual data
- Tesla plans to deploy Optimus robots for commercial use in 2026; Xpeng Robotics and Guangzhou Auto Group launching mass production of humanoid robots in 2026
- 2026 marks a breakthrough year with multiple commercial humanoid robot launches: Tesla Optimus, Xpeng Iron, GoMate, and 1X NEO entering commercial deployment
