# Ocean Calcium Decline Solved Long-Term Cooling Mystery

## Summary

University of Southampton researchers solved a 66-million-year mystery of how Earth transitioned from a greenhouse world to an ice-capped planet. Ocean calcium levels dropped over 50% in that period, reducing atmospheric CO2 and weakening the greenhouse effect.

## Key Findings

- Ocean calcium dropped >50% over 66 million years
- Reduced calcium altered ocean-atmosphere CO2 interactions
- Less CO2 weakened the greenhouse effect, enabling gradual cooling
- Evidence from fossilized sea creature remains in seafloor sediments
- Published in PNAS, January 9, 2026
