# Massive Runaway Stars in the Milky Way

ICCUB/IEEC/IAC study of 214 O-type runaway stars — the most extensive observational study to date.

## Key Findings

- Used Gaia mission data combined with IACOB spectroscopic project
- Most runaway stars rotate slowly; faster rotators likely linked to supernova ejections in binary systems
- Highest-velocity stars are typically single, supporting dynamical ejection from clusters
- Two competing ejection mechanisms: supernova kick in binary systems vs gravitational ejection during close cluster encounters
