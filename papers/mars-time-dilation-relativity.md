# Mars Confirms Einstein's Time Dilation

NIST scientists confirmed that clocks on Mars run faster than on Earth due to weaker gravity, as predicted by general relativity.

## Key Findings

- Mars surface gravity is about five times weaker than Earth's
- A clock on Mars runs ahead by ~477 microseconds per Earth day
- Over 50 years on Mars, a human would age 9 seconds more than on Earth
- Sub-microsecond delays can push navigation errors into tens or hundreds of meters
- Critical for future Mars satellite constellations and signal relay systems
