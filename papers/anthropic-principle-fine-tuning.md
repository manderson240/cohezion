## Abstract

The anthropic principle explains cosmological fine-tuning by proposing that the universe's fundamental constants appear precisely calibrated for life because we can only observe universes capable of supporting intelligent observers. This invokes multiverse scenarios where apparent fine-tuning becomes expected through selection bias.

## Key Findings

- The cosmological constant must be ~10^-120 joules/cm3; even 10x larger would prevent star and galaxy formation
- The weak anthropic principle states we shouldn't be surprised to find the universe compatible with our existence
- Multiverse hypothesis explains fine-tuning through selection bias—only life-permitting universes would contain observers to measure the constants
- Proponents argue this framework explains why physical constants are suited for complexity and life without requiring mathematical elegance
