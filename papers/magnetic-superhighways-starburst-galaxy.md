# Magnetic Superhighways in Starburst Galaxy Winds

## Summary

ALMA observations of Arp 220 revealed strongly magnetized molecular outflows creating "superhighways" that channel metals, dust, and cosmic rays out of the galaxy, showing magnetic fields are a crucial driver of galactic winds.

## Key Findings

- Nearly vertical magnetic field runs alongside bipolar molecular outflow at ~500 km/s
- Field strengths of 1-10 milligauss (100-1000x stronger than Milky Way)
- Ordered magnetic fields guide material into surrounding intergalactic medium
- Arp 220 is closest analog to extreme dusty star-forming galaxies in early universe
- Suggests strong organized magnetic fields may be common in high-redshift starbursts and regulate star formation across cosmic time
