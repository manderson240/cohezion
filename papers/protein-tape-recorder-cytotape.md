# CytoTape - Protein Tape Recorder for Cellular Activity

## Summary

University of Michigan researchers developed CytoTape, a flexible intracellular protein fiber that acts as a tape recorder for large-scale measurement of cellular activities. Like tree rings encoding physiological history, CytoTape records temporal cell activities along a protein fiber for post-mortem readout under conventional light microscopy.

## Key Findings

- CytoTape is a synthetic tape monomer introduced via gene delivery, forming intracellular protein fibers
- Records color-coded molecular tags corresponding to diverse cellular temporal signals over up to three weeks
- Does not alter normal cell physiology or mouse brain function in vivo
- Revealed unexpected temporal modes of regulatory pathways involved in cell plasticity
- Published in Nature
