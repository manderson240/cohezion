# MoM-z14 - Record-Breaking Distant Galaxy

## Summary

NASA's James Webb Space Telescope discovered MoM-z14, the most distant galaxy ever observed at redshift z=14.44, existing just 280 million years after the Big Bang. It is remarkably luminous and compact for its epoch.

## Key Findings

- Redshift z = 14.44, light from 280 million years after the Big Bang
- Mass of ~10^8 solar masses (similar to Small Magellanic Cloud)
- Only ~240 light-years across, 400 times smaller than the Milky Way
- 100 times more bright early galaxies than pre-JWST theoretical predictions
- Discovered May 16, 2025 by Rohan Naidu and 45 co-discoverers
