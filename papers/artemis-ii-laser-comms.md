## Abstract

Artemis II will test the first crewed laser communication system (O2O) on a lunar mission, transmitting data at rates over 100 times faster than traditional radio. The infrared laser system will transmit 4K video and mission data from lunar orbit, though a 41-minute blackout occurs when Orion passes behind the moon.

## Key Findings

- The Orion Artemis II Optical Communications (O2O) system uses infrared lasers to transmit voice, images, video, and science data at rates 100x faster than radio
- Artemis II will attempt to transmit 4K ultra-high-definition video and mission data across the Earth-Moon distance during the mission
- Future lunar missions could use orbiting relay satellites to eliminate the 41-minute communications blackout during moon passes
- This demonstration paves the way for laser communications systems at the Moon and Mars for future deep-space missions
