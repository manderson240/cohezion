# JWST Creates Sharpest Dark Matter Map

Diana Scognamiglio et al. at NASA JPL used JWST to create the most detailed dark matter map ever produced.

## Key Findings

- Twice as detailed as previous efforts, covering 2.5x the area of the full Moon
- Identified nearly 800,000 galaxies in constellation Sextans
- Dark matter detected via gravitational lensing — mass curves space, bending light from distant galaxies
- Wherever thousands of galaxies cluster, equally massive dark matter concentrations exist at the same location
- Thin filaments of ordinary matter between galaxy clusters have corresponding dark matter filaments alongside them
- Confirms dark matter and regular matter have co-occupied the same space throughout cosmic history
