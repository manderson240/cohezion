# AI Anomaly Detection in Hubble Archive

## Summary

ESA researchers David O'Ryan and Pablo Gomez developed AnomalyMatch, a neural network trained to search for rare astrophysical objects. The tool systematically scanned nearly 100 million image cutouts from the Hubble Legacy Archive in just 2.5 days, uncovering ~1400 anomalous objects, over 800 never previously documented.

## Key Findings

- First systematic search of the entire Hubble Legacy Archive for astrophysical anomalies
- Discovered merging/interacting galaxies with unusual morphologies and trailing streams
- Found gravitational lenses distorting background galaxy light into arcs and rings
- Identified galaxies with massive star-forming clumps and jellyfish galaxies with gaseous tentacles
- Detected edge-on planet-forming disks within our own galaxy
- Published in Astronomy & Astrophysics
