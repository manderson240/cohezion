## INVESTIGATION 1: Universes Team Research Fit

### Subquestion 1: What Are Universes Team's Core Problems?

**Hypothesis:** Universes team is likely focused on:
1. Training environments for agentic AI (providing diverse, well-structured tasks)
2. Agentic simulations (modeling agent behavior, planning, reasoning)

4. Interpretability & control (understanding and steering agent cognition)

**Why it matters:** FLUME is only valuable if it addresses real team needs.

   - Does FLUME enable new capabilities?

**Deliverable:** Mapping of Universes team needs → FLUME capabilities + top opportunities

## INVESTIGATION 2: FLUME's Unique Value Proposition

### Subquestion 1: What Can You Do With FLUME That's Hard Without It?

**Hypothesis:** FLUME enables capabilities that are difficult or impossible with standard RL.

**Why it matters:** Unique capabilities justify investment.

**Key Capabilities to Investigate:**
1. **Reasoning trajectory analysis** — Can we measure agent thinking quality?
2. **Reasoning interpolation** — Can we smoothly transition between reasoning approaches?
3. **Synthetic trajectory generation** — Can we train agents without real expert data?
4. **Reasoning transfer** — Can we port reasoning from one task to another?
5. **Adversarial reasoning evaluation** — Can we test reasoning robustness?
6. **Reasoning modification** — Can we surgically improve specific reasoning properties?

**Investigation Approach:**
For each capability:
1. **Design proof-of-concept experiment**
   - What would successful demonstration look like?
   - What metrics prove FLUME enables this?
2. **Estimate difficulty without FLUME**
   - How hard would it be to achieve this goal without FLUME?
   - What alternatives exist?
3. **Estimate value**
   - If we could do this, what research would it enable?
   - What problems would it solve?

**Example:**
Capability: Reasoning transfer (apply planning reasoning from Task A to Task B)
- Proof-of-concept: Train FLUME on planning tasks. Use reasoning embeddings to bootstrap agent on new task.
- Difficulty without FLUME: Very hard—would need complex curriculum learning or meta-learning
- Value: Enables rapid agent adaptation to new tasks, critical for multi-universe evaluation

**Deliverable:** Taxonomy of unique capabilities + value assessment for each
