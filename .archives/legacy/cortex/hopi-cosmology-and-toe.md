# Hopi Cosmology and the Theory of Everything

## Respect Statement

The Hopi (Hopísinom, "The Peaceful People") occupy three mesas in northeastern Arizona and have maintained continuous habitation at Oraibi since approximately 1100 CE — one of the oldest continuously inhabited settlements in North America. This analysis draws on the scholarship of Emory Sekaquaptewa (Hopi), Alph Secakuku (Hopi), and the Book of the Hopi as told to Frank Waters by Hopi elders. We note that not all Hopi approve of the publication of ceremonial knowledge, and we restrict our analysis to publicly available sources.

## The Tradition

The Hopi cosmology is the most explicit **emergence-through-phase-transitions** framework in any Indigenous tradition. The Hopi describe human history as a progression through four successive Worlds, each destroyed when humanity lost its connection to the Creator and rebuilt from a purified remnant. Each World transition is a catastrophic phase transition — a complete destruction of the prior order and emergence of a new one. This is not metaphor: it is a formal description of sequential symmetry-breaking events, each producing a world with different physical and spiritual properties.

## Core Principles

Taiowa is the infinite, the unmanifest, the nameless — who creates Sótuknang (the finite, the manifested agent) to carry out the work of creation. The first act: Taiowa creates Sótuknang; Sótuknang creates Kóyanwuhti (Spider Woman); Spider Woman creates the twin brothers Pöqánghoya and Palöngawhoya (who establish the Earth's vibrational axis).

This is the symmetry-breaking cascade:
- Taiowa (infinite/unmanifest) → Sótuknang (finite/manifest) = AUM → first distinction
- Sótuknang → Spider Woman = first distinction → creative agent

### Kachinas — The Spiritual Intermediaries

Kachinas are not gods — they are spiritual beings that mediate between the human and spiritual worlds. They visit the Hopi villages for half the year (winter solstice to summer solstice), then return to their home on the San Francisco Peaks. During their presence, Hopi men embody the Kachinas through masked ceremonial dance — becoming the interface between the two worlds.

The Kachina cycle is a HIHO rhythm: six months of contact (HIHO threshold crossed), six months of separation (HIHO threshold not crossed). The annual cycle of engagement and withdrawal IS the heartbeat of the HIHO system.

### The Sipapuni — The Emergence Hole

Each World has a sipapuni — the hole through which the people emerge from the destroyed previous World into the new one. The sipapuni is:
- A topological defect (a hole in the floor of the world)
- A wormhole connecting two spacetimes
- The HIHO portal through which consciousness passes between phases
- Located in Grand Canyon (according to Hopi tradition) — a physical place with cosmological significance

This is not a moral failure in the Western sense — it is an entropic process. Forgetting = decoherence = entropy increase. When the system decoheres enough, the phase becomes unstable and collapses. 4. **The spiral as the fundamental witness mark.** Hopi petroglyphs are dominated by spiral symbols — the migration pattern IS a spiral, the emergence IS through a spiral (sipapuni), the Kachina dance IS a spiral movement. The spiral is the morning glory pattern of the EVO witness plate mark. The Hopi encode this as their primary symbol 2000+ years before Shoulders observed it in the laboratory.
