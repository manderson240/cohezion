# Neutrino Physics

The study of neutrino properties, oscillations, mass ordering, and interactions. Neutrinos are electrically neutral, weakly interacting fundamental particles that come in three flavors (electron, muon, tau). The discovery that neutrinos have mass and oscillate between flavors is the first confirmed physics beyond the Standard Model, making neutrino physics one of the most active frontiers in particle physics and cosmology.

## Definition

Neutrino physics encompasses the experimental and theoretical study of neutrino properties: their masses, mixing angles, CP-violating phase, mass ordering (hierarchy), and interactions with matter. The field bridges particle physics (neutrino oscillations, double beta decay) and cosmology (neutrino contributions to large-scale structure, cosmic microwave background).

## Key Concepts

### Neutrino Oscillations

Neutrinos change flavor as they propagate — a quantum mechanical interference effect arising from the mismatch between mass eigenstates and flavor eigenstates. The transformation is governed by the Pontecorvo-Maki-Nakagawa-Sakata (PMNS) matrix, parameterized by three mixing angles (theta-12, theta-23, theta-13) and a CP-violating phase (delta-CP). See [[neutrino-oscillations]] for details.

### Mass Ordering (Hierarchy)

The three neutrino mass states have unknown absolute ordering. The "normal ordering" (m1 < m2 < m3) and "inverted ordering" (m3 < m1 < m2) produce different oscillation patterns. Determining the ordering is a primary goal of next-generation experiments including JUNO, DUNE, and Hyper-Kamiokande.

### CP Violation

If the CP-violating phase delta-CP is nonzero, neutrinos and antineutrinos oscillate differently. This lepton-sector CP violation could help explain the matter-antimatter asymmetry of the universe (baryogenesis via leptogenesis).

## Landmark Results (2025)

- **T2K-NOvA Joint Analysis** (October 2025): First-ever joint analysis of two long-baseline experiments, published in Nature. Provided the most precise measurement of the mass-squared difference (below 2% uncertainty) and constrained delta-CP
- **JUNO First Results** (November 2025): Within 59 days of first operation, JUNO measured solar oscillation parameters with 1.6x better precision than all previous experiments combined

## Key Experiments

| Experiment | Type | Primary Goal |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| T2K | Long-baseline accelerator (Japan) | CP violation, oscillation parameters |
| NOvA | Long-baseline accelerator (USA) | Mass ordering, CP violation |
| JUNO | Medium-baseline reactor (China) | Mass ordering with <1% precision |
| DUNE | Long-baseline accelerator (USA, under construction) | Definitive mass ordering, CP violation |
| Hyper-Kamiokande | Water Cherenkov (Japan, under construction) | CP violation, proton decay |
| IceCube | Atmospheric (South Pole) | Oscillations at high energies, sterile neutrinos |
