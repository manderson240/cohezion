## Definition

An Anthropic Research Engineer is a technical role at Anthropic focused on building, evaluating, and improving AI systems — particularly large language models in the Claude family. The role combines software engineering with AI research, encompassing work on model training infrastructure, evaluation frameworks, safety mechanisms, and developer-facing tools like Claude Code and the MCP protocol. Research engineers at Anthropic work at the intersection of ML research and production systems.

## Key Properties

- **Dual focus**: Combines research experimentation (evaluations, safety testing, capability measurement) with production engineering (scalable infrastructure, API design, developer tools)
- **Safety-oriented**: Anthropic's mission centers on AI safety; research engineers contribute to alignment research, constitutional AI, and responsible scaling
- **Tool builders**: Many research engineers work on developer-facing products (Claude API, Claude Code, MCP) that make AI capabilities accessible
- **Evaluation expertise**: Designing benchmarks and evaluation suites to measure model capabilities and safety properties
