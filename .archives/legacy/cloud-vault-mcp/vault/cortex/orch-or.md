# Orchestrated Objective Reduction (ORCH OR)

## Definition

Orchestrated Objective Reduction (ORCH OR) is a theory of consciousness proposed by physicist Roger Penrose and anesthesiologist Stuart Hameroff (1996, 2014). It proposes that consciousness arises from quantum computations in microtubules within neurons. The "objective reduction" (OR) is Penrose's proposal that quantum superpositions of spacetime geometry are inherently unstable and undergo spontaneous collapse when the gravitational self-energy of the superposition reaches a threshold — without requiring an external observer or decoherence.

The "orchestrated" part refers to biological microtubule networks orchestrating the quantum superpositions so that their collapse produces non-computable, coherent outputs — the subjective experience of consciousness.

## Key Properties

- **Objective Reduction (Penrose, 1989, 1994):** A quantum superposition of two distinct spacetime geometries (e.g., a mass in superposition of two positions) creates a "blister" in spacetime. This superposition is fundamentally unstable and self-collapses after a time:

> t ~ hbar / E_G

where E_G is the gravitational self-energy of the superposition (the energy required to gravitationally distinguish the two superposed geometries). For a nucleon displaced by its own radius: t ~ 10^7 years. For a 25-micron separation: t ~ 500 ms (conscious timescale).

- **Microtubules as quantum computers:** Microtubules are hollow cylindrical polymers of tubulin protein, 25 nm in outer diameter, found in the cytoskeleton of all eukaryotic cells. Each tubulin dimer can exist in two conformational states. In ORCH OR, these states form quantum superpositions that propagate along the microtubule lattice, performing quantum computation.

- **Decoherence challenge:** The primary objection to ORCH OR is decoherence — the warm, wet biological environment should destroy quantum coherence on femtosecond timescales (Tegmark, 2000, estimated 10^-13 seconds). Hameroff & Penrose responded that:
  - Microtubule interiors are shielded (ordered water, Debye screening)
  - Quantum effects in biology are increasingly documented (photosynthesis, avian navigation, enzyme catalysis)
  - Anesthetic gases selectively bind tubulin and abolish consciousness at the same concentrations that disrupt microtubule dynamics

- **Non-computability:** Penrose (1989, 1994) argued that human mathematical understanding involves non-computable processes, based on Godel's incompleteness theorems. Standard quantum mechanics is computable (unitary evolution + Born rule). OR introduces a non-computable element through the Penrose-Diosi gravitational collapse criterion.

## Mathematical Framework

### Gravitational Self-Energy

For a mass m in superposition of two positions separated by distance d, the gravitational self-energy is approximately:

> E_G ~ G * m^2 / d

For a more careful treatment using the mass density rho(x):

> E_G = integral integral [ G * (rho_1(x) - rho_2(x)) * (rho_1(y) - rho_2(y)) / |x - y| ] d^3x d^3y / 2

This reduces to the Newtonian gravitational self-energy of the difference mass distribution.

### Collapse Time

The characteristic time for objective reduction:

> tau = hbar / E_G

For a single tubulin dimer (m ~ 110 kDa, d ~ 0.4 nm):
- E_G ~ G * m^2 / d ~ 10^-52 J
- tau ~ 10^18 s (far too long for a single dimer)

For N ~ 10^9 tubulins in coherent superposition (a plausible cortical microtubule network):
- E_G scales as N^2 (if coherent)
- tau ~ 10^-2 s ~ 10 ms — consistent with the gamma oscillation frequency (30-90 Hz) observed in neural correlates of consciousness

### Penrose-Diosi Criterion

Diosi (1987, 1989) independently proposed that gravitational effects cause wave function collapse, with the collapse rate:

> Lambda = E_G / hbar

This is mathematically equivalent to Penrose's criterion and can be formulated as a modification to the Schrodinger equation:

> d|psi>/dt = (-i*H/hbar - Lambda/2) * |psi> + noise

The collapse term Lambda/2 causes decoherence-like effects but with a specific gravitational origin, distinct from environmental decoherence.

## Examples

- **Anesthetic action:** General anesthetics (xenon, sevoflurane, propofol) eliminate consciousness at concentrations correlated with their binding affinity to tubulin hydrophobic pockets. ORCH OR predicts this: anesthetics disrupt microtubule quantum coherence, preventing OR events.
- **Quantum biology evidence:** Long-lived quantum coherence observed in photosynthetic light-harvesting complexes (Engel et al., 2007) at physiological temperatures demonstrates that biological systems can sustain quantum effects longer than "wet and warm" estimates predicted.
- **Gamma oscillations (30-90 Hz):** Neural gamma oscillations are correlated with conscious awareness and binding of conscious percepts. ORCH OR proposes these oscillations are the macroscopic signature of OR events occurring at ~25 ms intervals.
- **Microtubule resonance:** Bandyopadhyay et al. (2013) reported resonance frequencies in single microtubules at megahertz and gigahertz frequencies, suggesting vibrational modes that could support quantum coherence.
