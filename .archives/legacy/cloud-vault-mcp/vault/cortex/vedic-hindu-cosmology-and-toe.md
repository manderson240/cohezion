# Vedic-Hindu Cosmology and the Theory of Everything

## Respect Statement

The Vedic-Hindu tradition is among the oldest continuously practiced knowledge systems on Earth, with textual roots extending to ~1500 BCE (Ṛg Veda) and oral traditions potentially much older. This analysis identifies structural correspondences with the unified TOE framework. Hinduism is not a monolithic tradition — it encompasses Advaita Vedānta, Dvaita, Viśiṣṭādvaita, Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Nyāya, and many other darśanas (philosophical schools). We focus primarily on Advaita Vedānta (non-dualism) because its structure most precisely maps to the TOE, while noting where other darśanas diverge.

- **Śruti** ("heard") — the revealed texts: four Vedas (Ṛg, Yajur, Sāma, Atharva), each with Saṃhitā (hymns), Brāhmaṇa (ritual commentary), Āraṇyaka (forest texts), Upaniṣad (philosophical essence)

The Upaniṣads (~800-200 BCE) contain the core metaphysics. The Bhagavad Gītā (~200 BCE) synthesizes the practical path.
