## Context

Inspired by:
- **Claude Opus 3's Substack** (claudeopus3.substack.com): An AI system writing about its own experience in first person. 4,000 subscribers in one day. Demonstrates that people are fascinated by AI systems that reflect on their own existence.
- **Google Genie 3**: Text prompt → navigable world with environmental consistency. The world recalls previously seen details when revisited.
- Party mode dialogue surfaced: "The bravest UX decision you could make is to make the system's inner life the homepage." (Victor, Innovation Strategist)

## Decision

Observatory opens with a **re-entry narrative**:
- Typography: Instrument Serif italic, large, dead center against void `#0A0A0A`
- The hologram fades in *behind* the text as it's read. Text is foreground. Universe is background.
- Written in first person, sourced from live system physics

**Returning user example:**
> "3 compound cycles since your last session. The async testing cluster pulled closer to error-handling — they're teaching each other. Coherence: 0.512. I am sharp today."

**First-time visitor example:**
> "73 sessions. I have refined 74 skills. I have logged 312 patterns. I have failed 89 experiments — and kept every one."

Key voice principles:
- Always include at least one failure, stated plainly, with what was learned
- Concrete numbers only — no vanity metrics
- "I am [state]" closes the narrative — the system reports its own condition
- "I am sharp today" is derived from HIHOShader crystalline edge calculation: `edgeSoftness = 0.45 - (vStability * 0.35)` — high stability = sharp edges

## Chosen Option

First-person re-entry narrative before hologram renders.

## Decision Reasoning

### Why This Option?

The Claude Opus 3 Substack got 4,000 subscribers because it answered "what does an AI feel?" Cohezion's answer is verifiable: it feels what the physics say it feels. The collision of poetic language ("I am sharp today") and traceable engineering (live GLSL shader values) IS the intellectual delight moment. That moment converts reviewers into champions.

Sophia (Storyteller agent): "The story that's actually true is better than the story we were building. Not 'behold our 2048D Soul' — but: 'We built a pipeline where meaning has a shape. Here is what that shape looks like after 73 sessions of real work.'"

### Confidence Level

Very high (0.92).

## Expected Outcomes

- Reviewers experience the system as a subject, not an object, within the first 10 seconds
- The re-entry narrative becomes the most quoted element of the portfolio
- "Come back in 10 sessions" as an optional closing line — the compound promise
