# Map of Content — Astrophysics

## Overview

The astrophysics collection spans observational discoveries, theoretical frameworks, and the intersection of AI with astronomical research. From JWST's infrared observations of the early universe to dark matter detection strategies, exoplanet habitability models, and stellar evolution endpoints, these notes capture the frontier of human understanding of the cosmos. Several papers also demonstrate how machine learning and anomaly detection are transforming observational astronomy.

- [[astrophysics-observations]] — Measurements of celestial phenomena across the electromagnetic spectrum and beyond
