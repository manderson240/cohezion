# Map of Content — Quantum Physics

## Overview

Quantum physics spans the vault's coverage of foundational quantum mechanics, practical quantum computing hardware, entanglement-enhanced sensors, exotic quantum states of matter, and the philosophical implications of quantum theory. These notes document both the theoretical landscape and the rapid experimental progress that makes quantum technologies increasingly relevant to Cohezion's AI and materials-science research interests.

- [[quantum-entanglement]] — EPR paradox, Bell inequality violations, and non-local correlations between particles
