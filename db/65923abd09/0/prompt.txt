# Enriching Agentic Journey Captures for Universe Models

## Context

Cohezion's compound engineering loop has sophisticated infrastructure for tracking agent journeys through the 12-parameter axiomatic universe, but the pipeline is operationally incomplete. The core measurement -- cohezion (the overlap between internal intent and external manifestation) -- defaults to 0.5 everywhere because nothing computes it. ### Ontological Foundation

The 12 parameters that define agent state emerge from successive application of the **quadrature principle** -- splitting awareness into orthogonal perspectives until we arrive at 4 fabrics, each with 3 observable dimensions:

| Fabric | Parameters | What It Captures |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Space** | spatial_x, spatial_y, spatial_z | Geometric position in possibility space |
| **Field** | physics, biology, field | Force interactions and domain coupling |
| **Control** | logic, quantum, control | Information flow, causation, entropy |
| **Precipitation** | temporal, novelty, precipitation | Manifestation of latent intent into reality |

**Spin** is the most basic principal component -- rotation + precession as the fundamental unit of information. When spin phases align, cohezion increases. When they diverge, symmetry breaks and new structure precipitates from the awareness of nothing.

**HIHO (Half-In-Half-Out)** is the stability principle: maximum cohezion occurs at exactly 0.5 overlap between internal state and external environment. This is not a constraint but an attractor -- the sweet spot where exploration and exploitation balance, where symmetry can break in either direction. All 12 parameters target HIHO at 0.5.

This plan closes the open loops progressively across 7 phases. Each phase is independently valuable and compounds into the next. The guiding principle: **the minimal observation that unlocks the most downstream value**.

**Formula**:

```python
metrics["coherence"] = sum(components) / len(components)
```
