## 📐 Universal Design Principles for Cinematic Simulators

### 1. The "Volume" Principle
Points are dead. Volume is alive. 
- *Technique*: Instead of drawing a point for an agent, draw a **volumetric falloff** using `AdditiveBlending`. Clusters of agents should naturally form "nebulae" of logic.
- *Application*: Map the "Coherence" dimension to the density of the volume.

### 2. Temporal Dilation Visuals
A simulation's time-step ($dt$) is a physical reality.
- *Technique*: Map $dt$ to visual distortion. High dilation (slow time) should feel heavy (chromatic aberration, vignette). Low dilation (fast time) should feel energetic (bloom, motion trails).

### 3. Hierarchical Focus
The user cannot track 1M agents simultaneously. 
- *Technique*: **Octree Pruning**. Only render agents in the high-fidelity frustum. Replace distant clusters with single representative "Super-Nodes" (Galaxies).

### 4. 12-Dimensional Mapping (The FLUME Palette)
