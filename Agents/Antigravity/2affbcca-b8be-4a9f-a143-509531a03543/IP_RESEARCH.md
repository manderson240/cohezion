# IP Research & Branding Risk Analysis

## 1. AMD Logo (Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.)
- **Status**: Registered Trademark.
- **Risk**: **HIGH**. AMD strictly controls its logo usage. Using the AMD logo as part of a third-party project's branding is a direct trademark violation unless explicit permission is granted.
- **Action**: **REMOVE** from the primary Cohezion logo. Replace with a generic "Performance Delta" (triangle motif).

## 2. Framework Logo (Framework Computer Inc.)
- **Status**: Registered Trademark.
- **Risk**: **MEDIUM-HIGH**. Framework is community-friendly but their "Gear" logo is their primary identifier. Using it in the Cohezion logo creates "likelihood of confusion" (people might think Cohezion is an official Framework project). 
- **Action**: **REMOVE** from the primary Cohezion logo. The "Lattice" background already pays homage to the hardware without infringing on the trademarked gear.

## 3. Tux (The Linux Penguin)
- **Status**: Open Copyright (Larry Ewing).
- **Risk**: **LOW**. Tux is free to use but often requires attribution for official use. Using Tux as a primary brand element makes Cohezion look like a "Linux distribution complement" rather than a sovereign platform.
- **Action**: **OPTIONAL / REMOVE**. We can mention Linux heritage in the Brand Book without including the mascot in the primary glyph.

## Final Recommendation: "Inspired Motifs"
To maintain a professional, sovereign identity for Cohezion, we will use **Inspired Motifs**—geometric shapes that evoke the spirit of our hardware roots without infringing on intellectual property.
