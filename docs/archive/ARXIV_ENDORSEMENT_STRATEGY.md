## Cross-Discipline Endorsement Path

### The Challenge
Your papers (FLUME, R-Zero) target **cs.AI** or **cs.CL** categories, but your formal credentials are in life sciences. arXiv requires endorsement from someone already publishing in that category.

### Strategy: Leverage Interdisciplinary Bridge

1. **Find Papers at Biology/AI Intersection**
   Search arXiv for papers that combine your expertise areas:
   - "Machine learning animal behavior"
   - "AI wildlife conservation"
   - "Deep learning ecology"
   - "Computational ethology"
   
   Example searches:
   - `arxiv search cs.AI + biology`
   - `arxiv search cs.LG + conservation`

2. **Check Endorser Status**
   For each relevant paper:
   - Go to the arXiv abstract page
   - Scroll to bottom → "Which of these authors are endorsers?"
   - Click to see if any can endorse for cs.AI

3. **Contact Potential Endorsers**
   Draft email template:
   ```
   Subject: arXiv Endorsement Request - CS.AI (Cross-discipline: Biology + AI)
   
   Dear Dr. [Name],
   
   I'm reaching out regarding arXiv endorsement for the cs.AI category. I read your 
   paper "[Title]" which bridges biology and AI - an intersection that aligns with 
   my own work.
   
   I hold an MSc in Primate Conservation from Oxford Brookes and BS in Animal Science 
   from Cornell. My current research applies continuous latent encoding methods 
   (inspired by Kyutai's CALM) to semantic reasoning in AI systems.
   
   If you're able to endorse, my arXiv endorsement code is: [CODE]
   Link: [ENDORSEMENT_LINK]
   
   I'm happy to share the draft paper for context.
   
   Best regards,
   Mike Anderson
   ```
