## §101 REJECTION RISK (Subject Matter Eligibility)

### Alice/Mayo Analysis (HIGH RISK)

**Step 1: Directed to Abstract Idea?**
- **YES.** Claims are directed to mathematical algorithms (neural network encoding, dimensionality reduction, loss function computation)
- Mental processes (semantic encoding, reasoning)
- Mathematical relationships (2048D→512D→12D compression, geodesic navigation)

**Step 2A: Judicial Exception?**
- **YES.** Claims recite:
  - Mathematical calculations (linear transformations, loss functions)
  - Mental concepts (semantic intent, reasoning, coherence)
  - Abstract data manipulation (encoding, compressing, projecting)

**Step 2B: Inventive Concept?**
- **WEAK.** Specification fails to demonstrate significantly more than abstract idea:
  - "Physics-grounded" is conclusory, not structural
  - 12D parameters are natural phenomena (Smith's 12 parameters are laws of nature)
  - HIHO 0.5 target is mathematical constant, not technical improvement
  - No specific hardware improvement claimed

### §101 Rejection Prediction

**Examiner will likely argue:**
> "Claim 1 is directed to the abstract idea of organizing information through mathematical transformation. The 2048D→512D→12D compression is a mathematical algorithm. The 12D 'physics-grounded' state merely appends a field of use to an abstract idea. The HIHO 0.5 target is a mathematical constant. No practical application transforms the abstract idea into patent-eligible subject matter."

### §101 Fix Strategy

**Required Amendments:**
1. **Add specific technical improvement:**
   - "wherein said 173× compression reduces GPU memory consumption by 99.4% compared to 2048D processing"
   - "wherein said physics-grounded 12D enables direct hardware sensor interfacing without semantic translation layer"

2. **Add concrete application:**
   - "wherein said Doer observable state directly controls robotic actuator via 12D-to-voltage conversion"
   - "wherein said trajectory prediction generates real-time control signals for autonomous vehicle navigation"

3. **Remove natural phenomena:**
   - Delete "Smith's 12-parameter reality framework" (laws of nature)
   - Replace with "12 learned dimensions trained on physics simulation data"

4. **Emphasize technical solution:**
   - "wherein said coherence loss eliminates vanishing gradient in deep VAE training"
   - "wherein said multi-scale architecture enables 10× faster inference than single-scale VAE"

## CLAIM CONSTRUCTION ISSUES

### Means-Plus-Function Risk (§ 112 ¶ 6)

**Problem:** Claim 1 uses "means" language implicitly:
- "means for encoding" (Knower Encoder)
- "means for compressing" (Thinker VAE)
- "means for projecting" (Doer Projector)

**Risk:** If construed as means-plus-function, claims limited to disclosed structure + equivalents.

**Fix:** Use structural language:
- "an encoder configured to..."
- "a variational autoencoder comprising..."
- "a projection layer having weights..."

### Jepson Claim Format

**Current:** All elements in preamble (no two-part form)

**Recommendation:** Convert to Jepson format for clearer novelty:
```
A multi-scale manifold encoding system comprising:
- a Knower encoder producing 2048D vectors (PRECHARACTERIZING);
- CHARACTERIZED IN THAT:
  - a Thinker VAE compressing said 2048D to 512D with HIHO coherence loss;
  - a Doer projector projecting said 512D to 12D physics-grounded state;
  - wherein said 12D state comprises four groups of three dimensions.
```
