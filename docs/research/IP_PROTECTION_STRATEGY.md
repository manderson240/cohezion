# IP Protection & Brand Strategy Research
## Protecting "Cohezion" as Intellectual Property

**Research Date:** February 2, 2026  
**Status:** Documentation Only - Legal Action Deferred  
**Priority:** Medium (pre-launch protection recommended)

---

## Executive Summary

"Cohezion" is a distinctive brand name for an agentic AI universe simulation platform. To protect this IP, we should consider:
1. **Trademark** (primary protection)
2. **Copyright** (automatic, for code/content)
3. **Patents** (for unique algorithms - optional)
4. **Trade Secrets** (for proprietary methods)
5. **Domain Strategy** (prevent cybersquatting)

---

## 1. Trademark Protection

### What Can Be Trademarked:
- **Word Mark:** "Cohezion" (text only)
- **Logo/Design Mark:** Visual logo (when created)
- **Tagline:** "Universe Simulation Platform" or similar
- **Sound Mark:** Audio logo (if created)

### Trademark Classes (USPTO):
For our platform, relevant classes include:

**Class 9:** Computer software, AI platforms, downloadable applications
- *"Artificial intelligence software for universe simulation and autonomous agent orchestration"*

**Class 42:** Scientific and technological services
- *"Software as a service (SaaS) featuring AI agent platforms"*
- *"Computer programming and software design"*

**Class 41:** Education and training
- *"Providing online educational materials in AI and simulation"*

**Class 35:** Business management
- *"Platform as a service (PaaS) featuring computer software platforms"*

### Geographic Coverage Options:

**Option A: US Only (Cheapest)**
- **Cost:** $250-$400 per class (USPTO TEAS Plus/Standard)
- **Timeline:** 8-12 months to registration
- **Duration:** 10 years, renewable indefinitely
- **Pros:** Cheapest, fastest
- **Cons:** Only protects in US

**Option B: International (Madrid Protocol)**
- **Cost:** $2,000-$5,000+ (depending on countries)
- **Coverage:** 120+ countries via WIPO
- **Timeline:** 12-18 months
- **Pros:** Global protection
- **Cons:** Expensive, complex

**Option C: Priority Markets**
- US, EU, UK, Canada, Australia
- Cost: ~$1,500-$3,000
- Covers major English-speaking markets + EU

### Trademark Search (Critical First Step):

Before filing, must search:
1. **USPTO TESS Database:** https://tmsearch.uspto.gov
2. **Common Law Search:** Google, social media, app stores
3. **International:** WIPO Global Brand Database
4. **State Registries:** Business name registrations

**Potential Conflicts to Check:**
- "Cohesion" (common word - likely not conflict)
- "Cohesion Software" (if exists)
- Similar AI platform names
- Domain name availability (cohezion.com, .ai, .io)

### Likelihood of Approval:

**Strengths:**
- Made-up spelling ("Cohezion" vs "Cohesion")
- Distinctive, not descriptive
- No obvious conflicts expected
- Unique concept (universe simulation + AI agents)

**Risks:**
- Phonetically similar to "Cohesion"
- May require "disclaimer" for descriptive elements
- Examiner might request clarification

**Estimated Success Rate:** 75-85% (good, not guaranteed)

## 2. Copyright Protection

### Automatically Protected:
- Source code (automatic upon creation)
- Documentation
- Visual designs
- Written content

### Registration (Optional but Recommended):
- **Cost:** $45-$65 per work (US Copyright Office)
- **Benefits:** Statutory damages in infringement cases
- **Timeline:** 3-9 months

### License Strategy:
```
Recommended: Dual License
├─ Open Source Core (AGPL or similar)
│  └─ Infrastructure, basic agents
└─ Commercial License
   └─ Enterprise features, proprietary algorithms
```

## 3. Patent Protection (Optional)

### Patentable Elements:
1. **12D/512D Manifold Projection Method**
   - Novel dimensionality reduction technique
   - Unclear if truly novel (transformer embeddings exist)

2. **HIHO Coherence Algorithm**
   - 0.5 coherence as stability attractor
   - May be mathematical principle (not patentable)

3. **Compound Engineering Methodology**
   - Self-reinforcing architecture pattern
   - Likely abstract idea (not patentable)

### Patent Costs:
- **Provisional Patent:** $1,500-$3,000
- **Full Utility Patent:** $15,000-$50,000+
- **Timeline:** 2-4 years

### Recommendation:
**Likely NOT worth patenting.**
- Algorithms may not be novel enough
- Expensive and time-consuming
- Open-source approach builds community
- Trade secrets better for proprietary methods
