## Notes
- Items 1–5 are additive/safe; 6–8 are behavior-changing (land behind the falsifiable check, else report-only).
- **Scope-expansion directive (2026-06-06):** the loop no longer terminates at a fixed list. Each tick, after completing one item, it APPENDS ≥1 new falsifiable item that extends a thread into adjacent territory (a new `Task` slot, an unused neuron `country`, a new measurement, a new resilience/telemetry check, or the next wiring package). New items MUST be additive + falsifiable + invariant-respecting + non-fabricated (research-gated ids stay TODO until the research loop verifies them). The loop only STOPS/notifies when scope cannot be expanded without a human decision (every frontier item is BLOCKED on human input).
