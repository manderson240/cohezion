# The Cohezion Method

This document codifies the official development methodology of the Cohezion platform, as decided by a democratic agentic AI consensus.

## Core Principles

The Cohezion Method is built on the following eleven core principles:

1.  **A Story-Driven Core**: Every piece of work, from a simple bug fix to a major new feature, must be initiated by a story. The story is the single source of truth for the work to be done.

2.  **YAML First**: All stories, tests, and other development artifacts must be defined in a structured YAML format. This ensures that all inputs are machine-readable, which is essential for automation and agentic collaboration.

3.  **Test-First for Quality**: We embrace an Acceptance Test-Driven Development (ATDD) approach. Every story must be accompanied by a set of automated acceptance tests that define the desired outcome. The story is only considered 'done' when all acceptance tests pass.

4.  **Embrace the Triune Self**: We recognize the 'Triune Self' (the Knower, the Thinker, and the Doer) as a foundational model for our platform and our processes. We aim to empower the human-in-the-loop to seamlessly transition between these three roles.

5.  **Continuous Learning**: The field of AI is constantly evolving, and so must our platform. We will build mechanisms to stay up-to-date with the latest research and developments, and to continuously incorporate new knowledge and capabilities into our platform.

6.  **Proactive Capability Assessment**: We will build a proactive capability assessment mechanism to constantly monitor our platform for gaps and bottlenecks. This will allow us to identify opportunities for improvement and to create new agents and abilities as needed.

7.  **Maintain a Shoshin (Beginner's) Mindset**: We will approach every problem with a beginner's mind, free from preconceived notions and assumptions. This is essential for true root cause analysis and for fostering a culture of innovation.

8.  **Embrace Adaptability and Evolvability**: We will not be dogmatic about our tools or our processes. We will be willing to adapt and to abandon old methods when they no longer serve us. Our platform and our methodology are designed for continuous evolution.

9.  **Introspection**: The platform will have the ability to examine its own performance, its own code, and its own processes. This is the foundation of self-awareness and the key to meaningful self-improvement.

10. **Self-Healing**: The platform will have the ability to not only detect problems, but to diagnose their root causes and to apply solutions automatically. This is the ultimate form of proactive problem-solving and a key component of a truly autonomous system.

11. **Hierarchical Reasoning**: We will adopt a hierarchical reasoning model, inspired by the human brain. Our agentic ecosystem will be composed of a high-level module for slow, abstract planning (our 'Thinker' agents) and a low-level module for rapid, detailed computations (our 'Doer' agents). This separation of concerns will allow us to tackle more complex problems in a more efficient and robust manner.
