## Typography

- **Body**: `'Inter', -apple-system, system-ui, sans-serif`
- **Display (numerals/headers)**: `'Inter', sans-serif`
- **Monospace (code/data)**: `'Space Mono', ui-monospace, monospace`

## When to use

Brutalist editorial / print-grade reports. Maximum contrast, no chrome, suited to whitepapers and architectural reviews.

## Surface coverage

Applied to all 5 Wave D2 dashboards:

- `cost-router-status.html` — model fleet + budget enforcer
- `journey-tracker-12d.html` — 12D agent journey radar
- `swarm-topology.html` — multi-agent orchestration map
- `flume-latent-explorer.html` — t-SNE latent space
- `compound-loop-traces.html` — 11-step pipeline traces
