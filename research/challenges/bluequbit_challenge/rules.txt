Challenge Rules

Participation in this Challenge constitutes your acceptance of these Official Challenge Rules. By joining this competition, you agree to all rules stated below.
1. Agreement to Rules

By participating in this Challenge, you acknowledge and agree that you have read, understood, and accepted these rules. Entry into the Challenge automatically constitutes acceptance of these rules.
2. Eligibility

Participants must be at least 18 years old or the age of majority in their jurisdiction. Participants may enter individually or as a team. All participants must comply with applicable laws and regulations.
3. Team Participation

    Teams may participate but must operate from a single registered account.

    Only submissions from the registered team account will be accepted.

    Participants may not register or submit from multiple accounts; doing so will result in immediate disqualification.

4. Submission Guidelines

    Submissions must comply with all instructions provided on the Challenge website.

    All submissions must be original works created during the Challenge period.

    Participants agree not to submit content that violates any applicable law or third-party rights, including intellectual property rights.

    The organizers reserve the right to request reproducible code, methodology, logs, or any supporting materials needed to verify the validity of a submission before awarding any prize. Failure to provide such materials may result in disqualification.

5. Data and Resources

    Participants may use publicly available data, open-source software, and resources, provided these are accessible to all participants without restrictions or significant costs.

    Participants may not use proprietary datasets, software, or tools unless explicitly allowed and made equally accessible to all participants.

6. Conduct and Disqualification

    Participants must conduct themselves professionally and ethically.

    Any form of cheating, plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration outside declared teams, or unethical behavior will result in disqualification.

    The organizers reserve the right to disqualify participants who violate these rules or who disrupt the Challenge process.

    To be eligible for any prize, participants must fully comply with all rules, instructions, and verification requirements.

7. Prizes

    Prize amounts, prize structure, and winning criteria will be listed on the Challenge website.

    A participant or team will only be eligible to receive a prize if their submission fully adheres to all Challenge rules and challenge-specific requirements.

    The organizers may require reproducible code, proof of work, a detailed explanation of the solution process, or other verification materials before confirming a winner.

    Failure to pass verification—at the sole discretion of the organizers—may result in disqualification and reassignment of the prize to another eligible participant.

    Prizes are awarded “as is,” and the organizers are not responsible for taxes, fees, or additional costs associated with receiving the prize.

8. Intellectual Property

Participants retain ownership of their intellectual property developed during the Challenge.

Participants grant the organizers the right to publicly share and promote submissions for marketing or promotional purposes.
9. Liability

    The organizers are not responsible for technical issues, submission errors, or lost submissions.

    Participants agree to release and hold harmless the organizers from any claims related to participation in the Challenge.

10. Privacy

    Personal information collected during the Challenge will be used solely for administration and communication related to the event.

    Participant information will not be shared with third parties without explicit consent, except as required by law.

11. Modifications and Cancellation

    The organizers reserve the right to modify, cancel, or suspend the Challenge at any time for any reason.

    Participants will be informed promptly of any significant changes via the Challenge website.

12. Governing Law

These rules and any disputes arising from participation in the Challenge are governed by the laws applicable in the jurisdiction where the organizers operate.
