# Advanced Physics Simulation

Computational modeling of unconventional and frontier physics phenomena including Exotic Vacuum Objects (EVOs), Low-Energy Nuclear Reactions (LENR), Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), and fractal toroidal geometries. These simulations explore regimes where standard models may be incomplete, using numerical methods to probe charge cluster dynamics, coherence thresholds, and non-equilibrium plasma states.

## Definition

Advanced physics simulation refers to the computational investigation of physical systems that lie outside mainstream textbook treatments -- phenomena where electromagnetic, nuclear, and gravitational forces interact in unexpected ways. This includes EVO charge cluster formation, LENR transmutation pathways, MHD dynamo models, and toroidal vortex structures that appear across scales from laboratory plasma to astrophysical jets.

## Key Properties

- **Multi-scale coupling** -- Phenomena span from nuclear (femtometer) to macroscopic (meter) length scales, requiring adaptive mesh or multi-resolution techniques
- **Non-equilibrium dynamics** -- Systems are far from thermodynamic equilibrium; energy densities can spike locally by orders of magnitude
- **Electromagnetic dominance** -- In many exotic regimes the electromagnetic force overwhelms gravitational and weak nuclear forces at condensed matter scales
- **Coherence thresholds** -- Collective quantum effects emerge when particle density and confinement conditions cross critical thresholds
- **Fractal toroidal geometry** -- Toroidal vortex structures with self-similar substructure appear as attractors in many simulation runs

## Examples

- **EVO (Exotic Vacuum Object) modeling** -- Simulating dense electron clusters that exhibit anomalous stability and transmutation capabilities, following Ken Shoulders' experimental observations
- **LENR lattice simulations** -- Modeling hydrogen loading in palladium lattices where anomalous excess heat and nuclear products have been reported
- **MHD plasma confinement** -- Simulating plasma flows in toroidal geometries relevant to both fusion energy and astrophysical jet formation
- **Fractal toroidal dynamics** -- Modeling nested toroidal vortices where energy cascades across scales in a self-similar pattern
