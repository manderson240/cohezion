## Definition

Materials science is the interdisciplinary field studying the relationship between a material's atomic structure, its processing history, and its macroscopic properties (mechanical, electrical, optical, thermal). By understanding structure-property relationships, materials scientists design new materials with targeted characteristics — superconductors, semiconductors, structural alloys, biomaterials, and photovoltaics.

AI is transforming materials discovery: machine learning potentials (trained on quantum chemistry calculations) enable molecular dynamics simulations at DFT accuracy but classical speed, accelerating the exploration of material space from years to days. Foundation models trained on materials databases can predict properties of hypothetical materials before synthesis. This accelerated discovery paradigm is the materials science analog of AlphaFold in structural biology.

For Cohezion's knowledge graph, materials science connects papers on DNA origami nanofabrication, amorphous material structure determination (3D atomic reconstruction), semiconductor patterning, and photonic nanocavities — a diverse set of methodologies unified by the structure-property-processing triangle.

## Key Properties

- **Structure-property relationships**: Atomic arrangement determines macroscopic behavior
- **Multi-scale**: Atomic (quantum chemistry) → nanoscale → microscale → macroscale interactions
- **Characterization methods**: X-ray diffraction, electron microscopy, spectroscopy, AFM
- **AI-accelerated discovery**: ML potentials and foundation models dramatically speed up property prediction
- **Cross-disciplinary**: Interfaces with physics, chemistry, biology, and engineering
