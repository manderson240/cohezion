# Black Holes

Regions of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing can escape. Key objects in observational astrophysics and tests of general relativity.

## Related Papers

- [[jwst-early-universe-black-holes]] — JWST discovered that supermassive black holes formed in the first 500 million years, potentially via direct gas cloud collapse rather than stellar evolution
