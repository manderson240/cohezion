## Definition

Discoveries from the James Webb Space Telescope, the most powerful infrared space observatory launched December 2021. JWST observations include detection of the most distant known galaxy JADES-GS-z14-0 at redshift 14.32 (290 million years after the Big Bang), supermassive black holes in the early universe, and atmospheric compositions of exoplanets including carbon dioxide and methane.

## Key Properties

- Observes in infrared wavelengths, enabling detection through dust and across cosmic distances
- NIRSpec spectrograph enables spectroscopic analysis determining composition and age of distant objects
- Discovered 300 candidate galaxies in early universe brighter than theoretical predictions
- Detected supermassive black holes as early as 440 million years after Big Bang
- Revealed atmospheric molecules (CO2, methane, water) in exoplanets

## Examples

- Galaxy JADES-GS-z14-0: most distant known galaxy at redshift 14.32, observed 290 million years after Big Bang
- Six 'universe breaker' galaxies dating to 500 million years post-Big Bang, each containing nearly as many stars as the Milky Way
