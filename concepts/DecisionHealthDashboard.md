# Decision Health Dashboard

A proposed UI canvas component for the GraphRAG visualization system. Displays the health and status of architectural decisions in the vault — showing decision staleness, consequence validation status, and dependency chains.

## Intended Features

- Decision age and review status
- Consequence tracking (predicted vs. actual outcomes)
- Dependency graph of related decisions
- Alert surface for decisions needing review
