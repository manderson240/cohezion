## Definition

Stable disruptions in fields arising from symmetry breaking in phase transitions, predicted by Tom Kibble in 1976. Domain walls, cosmic strings, monopoles, and textures persist because creation requires topologically non-trivial winding impossible to locally unwind. The Kibble-Zurek mechanism describes how causally disconnected regions choose different vacuum states, creating defect networks.

## Key Properties

- Form inevitably when systems break continuous symmetries in phase transitions
- Kibble-Zurek mechanism: causally disconnected regions choose different vacua, creating boundary defects
- Domain walls (codimension-1) and monopoles (codimension-3) are cosmologically catastrophic
- Cosmic strings (codimension-2) are viable, potentially seeding large-scale structure
- Persist because unwinding requires topologically non-trivial path through vacuum manifold

## Examples

- Early universe electroweak transition: Kibble mechanism creates domain walls between regions of different vacuum
- Cosmic strings may leave imprints on CMB as line-like features contributing to lensing
