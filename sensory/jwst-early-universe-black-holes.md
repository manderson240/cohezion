# JWST Revolutionizes Early Black Hole Understanding

## Summary

The James Webb Space Telescope has separated quasar light from surrounding starlight for the first time, revealing that the universe's earliest supermassive black holes may have formed without stars, via direct collapse of massive gas clouds.

## Key Findings

- JWST identified the earliest black hole yet: CAPERS-LRD-z9, existing just 500 million years after the Big Bang
- Standard stellar-mass black hole accretion models cannot explain observed supermassive sizes
- Direct collapse of huge gas clouds (without stellar intermediary) now confirmed as viable formation pathway
- JWST data supports decade-old theory of starless black hole formation
