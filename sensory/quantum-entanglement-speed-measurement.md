# Quantum Entanglement Speed Measurement

## Summary

Researchers at the Technical University of Vienna, with Chinese collaborators, measured the speed of quantum entanglement for the first time, revealing a ~232 attosecond lag between electron escape and the remaining electron's state change.

## Key Findings

- First-ever measurement of the temporal dynamics of quantum entanglement
- ~232 attoseconds between electron escape and entangled state change
- Used ultrabrief laser flashes (attosecond-scale) to control entanglement degree
- Implies potential for quantum processors operating at frequencies billions of times faster than current chips
