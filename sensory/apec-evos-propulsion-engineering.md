# Exotic Vacuum Objects (EVOs) & Propulsion Engineering

## Summary

Greenyer's foundational APEC presentation on EVOs as a propulsion technology, tracing the lineage from Shoulders through Bostick's plasmoids to practical interstellar drive concepts. Covers EVO structure, ball lightning connection, D4D fractal geometry, and propulsion mechanisms.

## EVO Properties for Propulsion

Based on Shoulders' and Greenyer's combined work, EVOs exhibit capabilities directly relevant to propulsion:

1. **Mass shielding** — ball lightning doesn't defy gravity; it shields mass from its effects
2. **Charge alteration** — particle charge can be modified (Shoulders challenged conservation laws)
3. **Inertia manipulation** — potentially enabling teleportation
4. **Material boring** — EVOs bore through materials via non-thermal processes
5. **Magnetic field generation** — fields potentially exceeding **1000 Tesla**, beyond Sun's core pressure

## Ball Lightning as EVO Manifestation

Dr. George Egely's observations:
- Ball lightning releases water or sand when disrupted
- Interior is not hot, but field of influence boils water and disintegrates matter
- Can **levitate significant weight** — shielding effect rather than anti-gravity
- Non-thermal nature: sand sintered at temperatures far below expected

Connected phenomena: Messiah's "Ektrons" (Russia), Matsumoto's "itonic clusters" — all converging on the same EVO phenomenon under different names.

## Propulsion Methods

Four propulsion mechanisms discussed:
1. **Mass ejection** — conventional but inefficient
2. **Mass rectification** — EVO-mediated
3. **Traction** — key to achieving light speed
4. **Dark matter distortion** — key to exceeding light speed

Eric Davis's research on ball lightning technologies supports potential for relativistic speeds. Simulations show expanding magnetic rings with extremely high internal fields moving at significant fractions of light speed.
