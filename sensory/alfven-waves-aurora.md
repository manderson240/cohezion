# Alfven Waves Power Earth's Auroral Displays

Researchers from University of Hong Kong and UCLA identified Alfven waves as the primary energy source behind Earth's auroras.

## Key Findings

- Alfven waves (plasma waves traveling along Earth's magnetic field lines) continuously transfer energy to the auroral acceleration region
- Confirmed via satellite data from NASA's Van Allen Probes and THEMIS mission
- Maintains the electric fields necessary for aurora formation
- Represents a universal model applicable to other planets and beyond
- Published in Nature Communications
