## Summary
The Milky Way's central bar is a gravitational engine channeling gas inward to trigger star formation bursts near the galactic core, while gradually reshaping the galaxy's structure and chemical gradients over billions of years.

## Key Abstractions
The bar acts as a conveyor belt feeding dense gas clouds toward the center, compressing them to ignite episodic starbirth. This internal fuel redistribution builds sharp chemical gradients across the disk and shapes distinct stellar populations — making the bar central to understanding the Milky Way's long-term evolution.
