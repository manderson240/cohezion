## Summary
Vector fields provide a framework for visualizing invisible forces — gravity, magnetism, wind — using arrows that show direction and magnitude at every point in space, bridging mathematical abstraction and physical intuition.

## Key Abstractions
Each arrow in a vector field encodes both direction and strength at a spatial coordinate, creating a continuous map of force or flow. Applications span planetary orbital mechanics, aerodynamics (airflow over wings), and fluid dynamics. The visualization technique transforms abstract differential equations into comprehensible spatial patterns.
