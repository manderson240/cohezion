## Abstract

Recent research reveals MCL1 as a direct controller of cancer metabolism through mTOR pathway modulation, while selective mTORC1 inhibitors can suppress MYC signaling and reprogram the tumor immune microenvironment. Scientists have also identified the molecular mechanism of MCL1 inhibitor cardiotoxicity and developed dietary interventions to mitigate this critical side effect.

## Key Findings

- MCL1 actively drives cancer metabolism by modulating mTOR signaling and hexokinase 2 (HK2) expression, linking survival to energy utilization
- Bi-steric selective mTORC1 inhibitors effectively reduce MYC protein levels and suppress oncogenic signaling without immunosuppressive effects
- Selective mTORC1 inhibition enhances rather than suppresses immunity, improving T cell and NK cell recruitment and synergizing with checkpoint blockade
- MCL1 inhibitor cardiotoxicity mechanism identified, with leucine supplementation successfully rescuing mTORC1 signaling in cardiac tissue
- Represents novel therapeutic opportunity combining metabolic and immune targeting in MYC-driven and MCL1-dependent cancers
