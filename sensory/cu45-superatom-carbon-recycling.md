# Cu45 Superatom for CO2-to-Ethylene Conversion

## Summary

Tsinghua University scientists created a stable 45-copper-atom superatom (Cu45) with closed-shell electron configuration, wrapped in protective organic molecules. It converts CO2 to ethylene with over 80% efficiency, surviving boiling water, concentrated acid, and temperatures up to 95C.

## Key Findings

- Cu45 nanocluster with closed-shell formation (noble-gas-like stability)
- Survived boiling water, concentrated acid, harsh chemicals, 95C heat
- Converted CO2 to multi-carbon (C2+) products: 81.8% selectivity
- 58% of product was ethylene (key industrial feedstock)
- Published in Journal of the American Chemical Society, January 2026
