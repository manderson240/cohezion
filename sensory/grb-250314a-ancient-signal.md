# GRB 250314A: Ancient Gamma-Ray Burst

NASA detected a 10-second gamma-ray burst from 13 billion light-years away, originating from a supernova when the universe was only 730 million years old.

## Key Findings

- Detected at Canberra Deep Space Communication Complex using a 70-meter antenna
- Among a tiny group of GRBs ever seen from the first billion years after the Big Bang
- Narrowband burst at a specific frequency, stretched by the expansion of space
- Classified as an "anomalous transient event"
- JWST confirmed the discovery ~3.5 months later by recording the fading afterglow
