# Exotic Vacuum Objects (EVOs), Transmutation & Anomalies

## Summary

Greenyer presents evidence of nuclear transmutation via EVOs, centering on Adamenko's "Ion Eater" SIMS experiments, gold synthesis from copper, and a simplified method for inducing transmutations using an ultrasonic cleaner.

## The Ion Eater Discovery

Stanislav Adamenko's device uses secondary ion mass spectrometry (SIMS) to analyze copper screens. The team observed:

1. **Black spots with zero secondary ion signals** — no known elements detected up to 480 AMU
2. **Primary ion beam disappearance** — the spots actively consumed the probe beam
3. **Flickering at 433 AMU** after the primary beam was switched off — exponential decay suggesting the spots were **actively capturing and assembling matter** into substances outside the known periodic table

This is documented in Adamenko's book *Controlled Nucleosynthesis*.

## Relativistic Discharge Experiments

Adamenko's experiments (300-700 joule relativistic discharges into metal targets):
- Formation of "electronucleus macroclusters"
- Bulk transmutation of elements
- **Gold synthesis**: spots with up to **76.3% gold from pure copper**
- Other spots rich in lanthanides
- Process is **self-sufficient** — produces far more energy than consumed
- Described as microscopic analog of natural explosive nucleosynthesis (supernovae energy source)

## Relic Neutrinos and Anti-Gravity

- Relic neutrinos (cold neutrinos) proposed as key to EVO formation and transmutation
- Alexander Shishkin's hydrodynamic generators produced novel penetrating radiation, possibly related to relic neutrinos
- Anti-gravity effects observed in association with EVO phenomena
- Three-body alignment effects (Sun-Moon-Earth) observed by Xu Wenzhou and Morris Alai — sideways object movement attributed to superfluid superconducting condensate of relic neutrinos

## Practical Replication

Suhas Ralkar's reactor: combination of corona discharge, ultrasonics, and specific resonant frequencies for nuclear transmutation.

**Simplified method**: ultrasonic cleaner + aluminum foil + deionized water → element synthesis. This dramatically lowers the barrier to experimental replication.

## Health Warnings

Greenyer cautioned about severe health effects experienced by researchers working with these technologies. The "strange radiation" documented after colleague Yuri's death was attributed to phenomena that some believed extracted life force.
