# 3D Atomic Structure of Amorphous Materials via AET

## Summary

Published in Nature, this research presents a quantitative framework for atomic electron tomography (AET) that enables reliable determination of 3D atomic coordinates and elemental identities in amorphous materials, overcoming the fundamental limitation that lack of periodicity poses for structural determination.

## Key Findings

- Amorphous materials lack long-range order, making 3D atomic structure determination extremely challenging
- AET provides a pathway to direct 3D atomic mapping
- Framework enables reliable determination of atomic coordinates AND elemental identities
- Underpins technologies from thin-film electronics to quantum technologies
