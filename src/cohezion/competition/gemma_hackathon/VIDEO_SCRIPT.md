# Gemma-4-Good Hackathon — 60-Second Demo Video Script

## "Compound Crisis Response: Local AI for Global Emergencies"

---

### Opening (0:00–0:08)
**Visual**: Map of the world with disaster icons (hurricane, earthquake, fire). Slow zoom into a rural area with no connectivity bars.

**Voiceover**: "When disaster strikes, the communities that need help most are often the ones with the worst internet."

---

### Problem (0:08–0:18)
**Visual**: Split screen. Left: hospital with paper logs and radio. Right: aid workers looking at blank phones.

**Voiceover**: "First responders waste precious hours relaying fragmented information. Central databases are unreachable. Lives are lost to communication gaps."

---

### Solution (0:18–0:35)
**Visual**: Screen recording of the Compound Crisis Response app. Agent receives query: "Hurricane Maria, Puerto Rico. Report local hospital status and triage needs." Gemma 4 generates a structured report. Offline, no cloud connection.

**Voiceover**: "Our system runs Gemma 4 entirely on local hardware. The Compound Loop queries local knowledge bases, validates alignment, and produces structured emergency reports — without ever needing the cloud."

**Visual**: Demonstrate multilingual support: English → Spanish. Then show the Kaggle kernel running.

**Voiceover**: "It works in Spanish, Haitian Creole, and English. Runs on a single Kaggle notebook. And adapts its skills based on real feedback."

---

### Impact & Scale (0:35–0:50)
**Visual**: Dashboard showing skill refinement over 8 episodes. Alignment score improving. Then show edge devices (AMD Ryzen AI, Ollama logo).

**Voiceover**: "Through experiential learning, weak skills are automatically identified and rewritten. The system gets smarter with every deployment. And because it runs on edge devices, it works where connectivity doesn't."

---

### Closing (0:50–0:60)
**Visual**: Split screen shows before/after. Before: chaos, paper, radio static. After: clean structured report on a laptop screen. Text overlay: "Compound Crisis Response — Powered by Gemma 4"

**Voiceover**: "Compound Crisis Response. Local intelligence for people who can't wait for the cloud."

**Text on screen**: 
- GitHub: github.com/cohezion
- Kaggle: kaggle.com/manderson240/gemma-compound-crisis-response
- License: Apache 2.0
