# CONCEPT: FLUME Methodology

## Overview
FLUME (Fluid Latent Understanding through Manifold Encoding) is the primary cognitive architecture for Cohezion swarm agents. It treats knowledge not as static data points, but as fluid trajectories through a latent manifold.

## Core Mechanics
1.  **Manifold Encoding**: Converting discrete information into continuous vector trajectories.
2.  **Fluid Understanding**: The ability of agents to "flow" between domains (Architect -> Engineer) without loss of context.
3.  **Trajectory Prediction**: Anticipating the evolution of a conceptual state based on historical manifold momentum.

## Evolution
FLUME evolves through **Continuous Experience Mining**, where structural markers in session logs are vitrified into new manifold constraints.
