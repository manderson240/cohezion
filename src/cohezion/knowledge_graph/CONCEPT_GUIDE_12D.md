# CONCEPT: 12D Latent Manifold

## Overview
Cohezion utilizes a 12-dimensional state space for all trajectory predictions and manifold encoding. This specific dimensionality was selected to avoid the "curse of dimensionality" found in higher-D spaces (like 37D) while maintaining sufficient complexity for high-fidelity physics.

## Dimensional Structure
- **3 Spatial Dimensions**: Traditional length, width, height.
- **1 Temporal Dimension**: The arrow of time and state transition history.
- **8 Brane Dimensions**: Specialized latent dimensions for specialized expertise (Architect, Engineer, Biologist, etc.).

## Mathematical Foundation
- **Vector Encoding**: Every thought or state is encoded as a 256-dim z-vector projected onto this 12D manifold.
- **Grounding Score**: The accuracy with which a high-level abstraction represents the underlying 12D complexity.
