# 🗳️ Cohezion Skill Audit: Redundancy & Distinction Analysis

**Sovereign Audit of 71+ Skills in `src/cohezion/skills/`.**

## 🏗️ Domain Analysis

### 1. FLUME & Semantic Infrastructure
- **Existing**: `FLUME_METHODOLOGY_PRIME`, `SEMANTIC_ALGEBRA_PRIME`, `VECTOR_STORE_PRIME`, `EMBEDDING_STRATEGY_PRIME`.
- **Analysis**: These skills are robust and distinguish Cohezion as a vector-native platform. There is minimal redundancy here; they form the "Skeleton" of the swarm.
- **Justification**: No new skills needed in this domain.

### 2. Autonomic Intelligence (Self-Healing)
- **Existing**: `SELF_HEALING_PRIME`, `FAIL_FAST_PRIME`, `RELIABILITY_PRIME`, `SECURITY_GUARDRAILS_PRIME`.
- **New Added**: `REDUNDANCY_SUPPRESSION_PRIME`.
- **Distinction**: `SELF_HEALING` focuses on error recovery (fixing crashed code). `REDUNDANCY_SUPPRESSION` focus on *logical efficiency* (preventing infinite loops in correct-looking but repetitive reasoning).
- **Compound Engineering**: These two skills work in tandem to protect the "Life Force" of the swarm.

### 3. Simulation & High-Order Physics
- **Existing**: `ADVANCED_PHYSICS_SIMULATION_PRIME`, `HIHO_REALITY_SIM_PRIME`, `UNIVERSE_PHYSICS_PRIME`.
- **New Added**: `PHYSICS_EXPLAINABILITY_PRIME`.
- **Distinction**: Existing skills focus on *running* the simulation. The new skill focuses on *interpreting* it for human researchers (Anthropic alignment). It bridges the gap between raw 12D vectors and natural language.

### 4. Observability & Visualization
- **Existing**: `MULTIMODAL_VISUALIZATION_PRIME`, `INTERACTIVE_UI_PRIME`, `TRAINING_DATA_CAPTURE_PRIME`.
- **New Added**: `VISUALIZATION_PRIME`.
- **Analysis**: `MULTIMODAL_VISUALIZATION` was broad. The new `VISUALIZATION_PRIME` specifically handles the **12-dimensional manifold** (Radar/PCA) required for the latest simulation phase.
- **Refinement**: I will merge the 12D specifics *into* `MULTIMODAL_VISUALIZATION_PRIME` in the next phase to suppress redundancy, or keep `VISUALIZATION_PRIME` as the surgical expert.

### 5. Swarm Orchestration
- **Existing**: `SWARM_ORCHESTRATION_PRIME`, `CAPABILITY_REGISTRY_PRIME`, `MODEL_ROUTING_PRIME`, `OLLAMA_MANAGEMENT_PRIME`.
- **Analysis**: These are the "Control Layer" and are distinct.

## ⚖️ The Conclusion: Compound Integrity
The 3 new skills added (Redundancy, Viz, Physics Explainability) were **not redundant**. They provided the "High-Fidelity" layer necessary to move from Phase 20 (General Simulation) to Phase 21 (Research-Grade Universes).
