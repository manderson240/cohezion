**A Multi-Layered Substrate for Autonomous Agentic Research.**

## 🗺️ Architectural Layers

### 1. The Semantic Substrate (FLUME)
*The Core of Latent Meaning.*
- **Technology**: `FlumeEncoder` (Z-Vector Projection).
- **Function**: Projects every agent thought into a **12-dimensional PhysicsState**.
- **Dimensions**: Coordinates (X, Y, Z), Time, Mass, Sentiment, Complexity, Factuality, Connectivity, Stability, Novelty, and Coherence.
- **Research Impact**: Enables mathematical modeling of reasoning "trajectories" across the manifold.

### 2. The Autonomic Swarm (Registry & Agents)
*The Engine of Agency.*
- **Technology**: `LocalRegistry` & `BaseAgent` with surgical skills.
- **Protocol**: **R-Zero Methodology** — a co-evolutionary loop between the environment (Challenger) and the agents (Solvers).
- **Protection**: Native **Redundancy Suppression** (Entropy-based) and **Circuit Breakers** built into the substrate.

### 3. Persistence & Graph (SurrealDB)
*The Collective Memory.*
- **Technology**: SurrealDB (Schema-full/Graph/Vector).
- **Interface**: `SurrealClient` with **Persistence Guard**.
- **Data Model**: `UniverseNode` (ID, Content, Embedding, PhysicsState, Metadata).
- **Insight**: Every "Experience" is atomized and queryable, providing 100% auditability of the agent's journey.

### 4. Observability & Explainability
*The Research Window.*
- **Technology**: Marimo (Interactive Notebooks) & Pocket TTS.
- **Tools**: PCA Trajectory Maps, 12D Radar Clusters, and the **Physicist Narrative Agent**.
- **Narrative**: The system translates raw vector variance into natural language (e.g., "Complexity spike detected") to ensure human-agent alignment.

### 5. Gateway Security & Guardrails
*The Ethical Perimeter.*
- **Technology**: `PromptGuard` & `OutputFilter`.
- **Mission**: Zero-trust approach to agentic execution. All inputs and outputs are filtered for toxicity, PII leakage, and adversarial patterns.

## 🔄 Dynamic Resilience: The HIHO Protocol
Cohezion defines stability as **Half-In-Half-Out (HIHO)** coherence. When an agent's coherence falls below 0.5, the system triggers autonomic perturbations to restore homeostasis, preventing the "hallucination spiral" common in standard frameworks.
