# Cohezion Product Guidelines

## 1. Tone and Voice: The Triune Communication Model
The platform's voice mirrors its architecture, operating simultaneously across three distinct registers:
*   **The Knower (Visionary & Immersive):** High-level documentation, charters, and onboarding materials must utilize a philosophical, narrative-driven tone. We speak of EVOs, the Triune Manifold, and the "amalgamation of all that has come before us" to immerse the user in the scale of the undertaking.
*   **The Thinker (Academic & Rigorous):** Specifications, BMAD-METHOD plans, and scientific outputs (Quarto) must be highly academic, focusing on rigorous mathematical proofs, equations, and explicit adherence to the 0.5 Coherence Rule.
*   **The Doer (Direct & Brutal):** During execution and incident response, the system must communicate with uncompromising efficiency. Errors are presented without sugarcoating, emphasizing velocity and reality-grounding over politeness.

## 2. User Experience (UX) Paradigm: The Layered Interface
The interface is a seamless synthesis of three distinct interaction models, allowing the user to zoom in and out of the substrate at will:
*   **Immersive Visualization (The Macro):** The primary view is a 3D/fluid visualization of the 12D/2048D manifold, allowing users to physically witness EVO trajectories, charge clusters, and the stabilization of COHEZION in real-time.
*   **Telemetric Dashboards (The Meso):** A high-density layer providing critical vitals (coherence scores, Phi scores, UCP transaction rates) for rapid assessment of swarm health and economic state.
*   **Living Documents (The Micro):** Reactive Marimo and Quarto notebooks serve as interactive, deep-dive environments. These allow humans to engage in live-coding alongside SOTA SLMs, interacting directly with the codebase and agent logic.

## 3. Transparency & Telemetry: Embracing the "Wall of Red"
Cohezion maintains radical transparency regarding failure, viewing it as the primary engine for evolution. This is presented in a multi-faceted manner:
*   **Raw Substrate:** The "Wall of Red" (raw error logs, stack traces, and unstructured system noise) is always accessible. We do not hide our mistakes.
*   **Curated Telemetry:** High-level metrics allow operators to monitor degradation thresholds and system-wide coherence without being overwhelmed by the raw noise.
*   **HITL Translation:** The Human-in-the-Loop Context Coordinator can dynamically translate raw failures into actionable "lessons" and abstract insights, bridging the gap between machine failure and human strategy.

## 4. Visual Aesthetic: Fluid & Organic
The guiding visual aesthetic across all interfaces—from the multimodal web app to the Obsidian Vault—must be **Fluid and Organic**.
*   **Design Language:** Interfaces should mimic the continuous, self-organizing nature of plasma fields and the FLUME methodology. 
*   **Styling:** Utilize smooth gradients, soft transitions, and organic shapes. Avoid rigid, blocky "industrial" designs. The UI should feel like a living, breathing entity that shifts and flows in response to the swarm's activity and coherence state.
