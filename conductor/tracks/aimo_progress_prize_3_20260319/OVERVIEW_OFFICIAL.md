# Official Overview: AI Mathematical Olympiad - Progress Prize 3

## Description
The AI Mathematical Olympiad (AIMO) Prize is a $10 million prize fund to spur the development of AI models that can reason mathematically. This competition is the third Progress Prize.

The goal of this competition is to create AI models capable of solving difficult math problems. The problems are similar to those found in the American Invitational Mathematics Examination (AIME) and the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO).

## Evaluation
Submissions are evaluated based on their accuracy in solving a set of math problems. Each problem has a non-negative integer answer. The score is the number of problems solved correctly.
